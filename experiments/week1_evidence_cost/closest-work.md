# Closest work and scope of the current mechanism

The following primary sources were read on 2026-09-21. These are conceptual comparisons, not performance measurements or novelty adjudications.

- [Build Systems a la Carte](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2018/03/build-systems.pdf), Mokhov, Mitchell and Peyton Jones (2018): separates scheduling from rebuild decisions and studies changed dependencies, dirty information and reuse. Our inference: S is an ordinary coarse version/dirty cache. Because the public context embeds global ledger versions, unrelated updates currently require invalidation under exact-output equivalence. No new incremental-computation principle has been demonstrated.
- [Proof-Producing Congruence Closure](https://www.cs.upc.edu/~roberto/papers/rta05.pdf), Nieuwenhuis and Oliveras (2005): recovers explanations for equalities while maintaining efficient equivalence/congruence reasoning. Our system is not term congruence closure; execution evidence, withdrawals and needs_review have different contracts. The shared idea of efficient queries alongside recoverable justification cannot by itself establish novelty. The paper does not validate our scientific claims.
- [Provenance Semirings](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf), Green, Karvounarakis and Tannen (2007): annotates relational results, using addition for alternatives and multiplication for joint contribution. Boolean instances can express OR/AND; general polynomial provenance also retains multiplicity. Our inference: a flat support-root set cannot express both alternatives and grouped conjunctive premises. Adding typed groups would be explicit semantic/schema work, not a caching innovation. Positive relational provenance alone does not supply our withdrawal/refutation/review policy.
- [DiscoveryBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01725): supplies data, metadata and research questions grounded in published research workflows. Our short official-client runs used real public TRAIN inputs, but are neither official scoring reproductions nor evidence that two execution-status claims represent the intended evidence workload.

Current decision: no C has a supported distinction from S or a meaningful observed bottleneck. The later domain-rich-r2 workflow observed 14 program-reported statistics and one declared natural dependency, with exact source binding and preserved scientific uncertainty. Its native R/B/S replay also observed zero actual cache hits. This closes the specific missing-workload observation, without extending the result to arbitrary long-horizon evidence graphs.
