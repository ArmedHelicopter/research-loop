Cross-cutting placement: C4 currently creates its summary descriptors inside `run_stage` (`full_loo_driver.py:74-111`) before `ArtifactCatalogue.seal` (`:131-142`), while semantic replay happens in `verify_stage` (`:172-267`). Each module bridge belongs at its own durable writer (JSONL append, phase file/event write, or builder output write) after the source write is durable; its verifier belongs in `verify_stage` after catalogue verification and before reconstructed `prepare`/`run_phase`/build. This avoids treating one final descriptor as coverage of unseen intermediate, disabled, or failed outputs.
