# C4 full composition and matched controls

`research_loop.modular.full_loo_composition` freezes the prospective C4 recipe.
It retains full F, every requested F-Mi, the structural `without-M2` row (M3
and M9 require M2), B0, and a distinct ordinary useful control.  For the
two-task TRAIN engineering slice this is 11 executable procedures, 22 target
cells, 9 deduplicated candidate builds, 45 history/build and 124 target model
opportunities (169 total), 62 independent source qualifications, 87 fixed
retrieval requests, 80 restricted Docker opportunities, and 22 independent
scorer opportunities.  Full, every legal LOO, and the strong control each have
six target model slots; B0 is explicitly an unequal two-slot reference.

Each module records its history/build and target boundary.  M9 is history-only;
all other modules have an explicit level at both boundaries.  Whole-pipeline
LOO clears every in-scope level.  Candidate sharing requires the complete
history/build factor map, candidate procedure, and exposure class to match.
Their target procedure is deliberately excluded: B0 and the ordinary control
may share their identical all-off ordinary history candidate, while an M8-only
target change shares with full.  Any history/build change receives its own
predeclared candidate recipe.

B0 and the ordinary control are separate procedures.  The ordinary control
has ordinary planning, retrieval, two critiques, execution, and revision under
the same resource schedule.  Those operations are deliberately named and
checked separately from M1-M9, so a control does not acquire an on-module
operation merely to satisfy a common output schema.

The plan contains no validation capability and declares no scientific effect.
It is a freeze/assembly seam; actual restricted builders, Docker execution,
independent scorer processes, native journal replay, and C1-C5 coverage remain
required before any execution receipt can support an engineering claim.
