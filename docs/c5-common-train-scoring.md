# Common C5 TRAIN scorer boundary

`JointTrainPanel` binds the complete new common-procedure catalogue to every
frozen TRAIN target, the common scorer and predeclared ranking rule. Its identity
includes the complete protocol, candidate packages, original build receipt
identities and runtime/barrier digests. Canonical history sharing is scoped by
the full protocol digest, and identical histories must retain one package.
History-only package exposure, all matched candidates, the separate B0 reference
and structural exclusions remain explicit. B0 is outside the ranking tie order.

The independent scorer process accepts this panel only through the new
`c5-common-train-scorer-process-config-v1` schema and `joint_train=True` client
scope. C4 and other family parsers keep their original contracts. Startup binds
the full panel, scorer, task handles and separate execution/scoring keys. It
reuses the existing signed candidate transaction, private reference resolution,
UTF-8 transport and no-retry journal. Validation is rejected by the panel and
worker before evaluation.

The integration check starts a real separate scorer process and obtains signed
adapted scores for synthetic candidates from both primary benchmarks. It checks
the closed scope and private-reference boundary. The candidate signatures and
build identities in this check are synthetic; they do not establish actual
module execution, complete common-grid execution or scientific efficacy.

Panel construction verifies a contract, not original history/build/runtime
files. The owning C5 controller still must execute and replay those originals,
bind independently versioned component consumers, seal the complete common grid
and authenticate selection. This change does not grant validation access,
scientific acceptance or deployment authority.
