# Useful review controls for lineage and admission panels

The seven existing lineage/admission panels retain their complete registered
grids, dependencies, source qualification, materials, scoring and budgets. A
new explicit v3 primary-TRAIN configuration freezes a useful-output review
recipe into each scenario. Legacy v1/v2 behavior and results remain unchanged.

Both matched review outputs reach the solver in every cell. When M5 is off,
the second review may consume the first as ordinary sequential revision. When
M5 is on, both contexts are sealed and the actual ReviewEngine submission
barrier remains in force. Ordinary comments never become validated evidence.
Every arm still has four model slots, one Docker attempt, one scorer opportunity
and the same unchanged M1/M2/M3 transition and public-context byte limit.

This applies to M2/M3, M2/M5, M3/M5, M2/M3/M5 and M1/M2, M1/M3, M1/M5.
The M2/M3 and M2/M3/M5 dependency restrictions remain: six and twelve core-pair
cells respectively, with no invented missing cells or identifiable full
interaction. The seven panels together retain 58 actual legal cells.

Docker and independent scorer processes. Replay must bind ordinary reviews to
their original requests/responses, reject blinded-context contamination and
preserve missing-cell and unknown-usage handling. References stay in scorer
processes. No actual effectiveness, calibration or validation acceptance follows
from these engineering grids; all remaining pair/triple/full/LOO obligations stay
open.

both actual review records. The new recipe is bound by the v2 scenario and v3
configuration, which change the panel digest without altering scorer criteria.
